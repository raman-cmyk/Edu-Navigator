# 00 — Overview

## The problem

A Nepali student wanting to study abroad has two sources of information, both broken.

**Consultancies.** The counselor recommends whichever university pays the highest commission and never discloses it. The student commits NPR 30-60 lakh — usually against family land — based on advice designed to sell.

**Facebook groups and Reddit.** Unmoderated, and a large share of "students" answering are agents in disguise. No way to tell who's real.

There is no trustworthy, searchable, structured place. That's the gap.

## The product

**Baato** (बाटो — path). Three parts.

### 1. Verified community

A forum where every contributor is verified by document. Badges show exactly who someone is: `Gold · Melbourne · Deakin '24`.

The mechanic: **anyone can ask, only verified students can answer.** Agents are permanently labeled and cannot post.

### 2. Honest shortlist tool

Free, no signup. Seven questions in. Ranked universities out — with real total cost in NPR, honest visa odds, PR pathway, and **the exact commission Baato earns from each**.

It also names universities to avoid, with the commission it's forgoing by saying so.

### 3. Manual ops (not software)

Three humans do the actual consultancy work: SOPs, documents, NOC runs, visa prep. Flat published fee, ~40% below consultancies. They log every task in the friction log — that log picks V2, not this document.

## Why it wins

Consultancies cannot publish commissions. Their entire margin is the hidden number. The moment they disclose, the model breaks.

And they cannot fake thousands of verified alumni telling the truth in public. The AI and the app are copyable. The community is not.

## The flywheel

```
Seeker researches
   ↓
Gets honest answers from verified alumni
   ↓
Uses the service (trust already exists)
   ↓
Goes abroad → verifies → answers next batch
   ↓
Contributes structured cost/salary data
   ↓
Shortlist tool gets more accurate
   ↓
Attracts more seekers
```

Community is the product. Consultancy is the monetization.

## Users

| Role | Description | Primary need |
|---|---|---|
| Seeker | 18-26, in Nepal, considering abroad | Honest answers, no sales pressure |
| Applicant | Actively applying | Peer proof + someone to do paperwork |
| Landed | In-country, 0-2 years | Housing, jobs, community, status from helping |
| Alum | 2+ years, working or PR | Recognition, reputation, giving back |
| Parent | 45-60, pays | Nepali language, proof it isn't a scam |
| Agent | Consultancy staff | Will infiltrate. Label, don't ban. |

**Design for Landed and Alum.** They generate the content. Seekers are the market. Design decisions that trade alumni experience for seeker convenience are wrong.

## V1 scope

### In

- Verified community: 5 stage rooms, 4 city rooms, threads, answers, search
- Verification flow with manual human review
- Shortlist tool (public, no auth) + shareable result
- Bilingual UI (Nepali/English)
- Viber + email notifications
- Admin: verification queue, moderation queue, friction log, data console

### Out — deliberately cut

SOP generator · document OCR · visa simulator · loan marketplace · forex · parent portal · roommate matching · airport pickup · buy/sell · job board · IELTS prep · payments · any country beyond Australia · any city beyond the four

Each is on the long list. None is now. The friction log decides which comes first.

## Geography

**Australia only.** Four cities: Sydney, Melbourne, Adelaide, Brisbane.

Adding Toronto with six users makes the whole product look abandoned. Depth beats breadth.

## Success metrics

| Metric | Month 6 | Month 12 |
|---|---|---|
| Registered | 5,000 | 25,000 |
| Verified | 500 | 3,000 |
| Posts/day | 50 | 300 |
| **Answer rate** (verified answer <24h) | **60%** | **80%** |
| Shortlist runs | 3,000 | 20,000 |
| Students placed | 0 | 100 |

**Answer rate is the metric that matters.** If a scared student asks at 2am and a verified alum answers by morning, the product works. If not, nothing else saves it.

## Phases

**Phase 0 — Seed (months 1-3).** Nothing public. Recruit 100 verified alumni manually. Get 300 honest posts in before launch. A community that opens empty dies and doesn't come back. Not optional.

**Phase 1 — Open (months 4-6).** Public launch, community + shortlist. Nothing to sell. Distribution via Nepali-language YouTube and TikTok.

**Phase 2 — Monetize (months 7-12).** Three-person consultancy turns on. Commission transparency announced publicly — that's the launch story.

**Phase 3 — Decide (month 12+).** Raise with proof. Build V2 from the friction log.
